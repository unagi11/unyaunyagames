Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Overview

UNYAUNYA GAMES respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information for our games and related support pages.

Games Covered

This policy applies to Escape! House of Bonds (Korean: 탈출! 인연의집, Japanese: 脱出！絆の家) and other games published by UNYAUNYA GAMES unless a separate policy is provided.

Information We Collect

Our game does not require an account, login, online chat, or user-generated content. We do not intentionally collect personal information through the game.

The game saves progress, settings, and audio preferences locally on your device. When a platform cloud-save feature is available and enabled, save data may also be stored in the cloud account provided by that platform.

UNYAUNYA GAMES does not operate a separate game server and does not receive or access your cloud-save data.

Third-Party Services

The game does not currently use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login, or in-app purchase systems.

Depending on the platform, the game may use Apple Game Center and iCloud, Google Play Games, or Steam for achievements, platform authentication, and cloud saves. These services are provided by the platform operator and may process platform account identifiers, achievement progress, and save data according to their own privacy policies.

UNYAUNYA GAMES does not request server-side access to platform accounts and does not combine platform data with data from other services.

Children

Our game is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us by mistake, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at unyaunyagames@gmail.com.
